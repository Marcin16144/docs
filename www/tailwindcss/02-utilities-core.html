Spacing — padding i margin

Tailwind uzywa skali liczbowej, w ktorej 1 jednostka = 0.25rem (4px). Wartosci: 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, 2.5, 3, 4, 5, 6, 8, 10, 12, 16, 20, 24, 32, 40, 48, 56, 64, 72, 80, 96.

KlasaWartosc
p-0padding: 0
p-1padding: 0.25rem (4px)
p-4padding: 1rem (16px)
px-6padding-left/right: 1.5rem (24px)
py-3padding-top/bottom: 0.75rem (12px)
m-2margin: 0.5rem
mx-automargin horizontal auto (centrowanie)
-mt-4negative margin-top -1rem
space-y-4vertical gap miedzy dziecmi przez ::not(:last-child)
gap-4gap dla flex/grid

Kolory

Tailwind ma palety: slate, gray, zinc, neutral, stone, red, orange, amber, yellow, lime, green, emerald, teal, cyan, sky, blue, indigo, violet, purple, fuchsia, pink, rose. Kazda ma 11 odcieni: 50, 100, 200, 300, 400, 500 (default), 600, 700, 800, 900, 950.
